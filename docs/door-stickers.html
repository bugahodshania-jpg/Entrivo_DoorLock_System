To print: press Ctrl+P and choose A4, portrait, 100% scale, and turn on "Background graphics". Each sticker is 80 mm wide. Cut on the outline.

Outside

Go in and lock

To go in

  1. Put your finger on the scanner, or tap your card on the card reader.
  2. Wait while the system verifies you and your assigned schedule.
  3. Listen for the voice: "Access granted."
  4. Open the door.

To lock with a card

  1. Tap your card on the card reader inside the room.
  2. Go out and close the door.
  3. Touch the touch sensor. The door locks.

To lock with your finger

  1. Go out and close the door.
  2. Touch the touch sensor.
  3. Put your finger on the scanner within 15 seconds. The door locks.

Emergency (no internet)

  1. Tap the emergency card on the card reader outside the door. The door opens.
  2. To lock, follow the steps above.
Touching the touch sensor only will not lock the door.
Inside

Go out and lock

If you have a card

  1. Tap your card on the card reader inside the room.
  2. Listen for the voice.
  3. Go out and close the door.
  4. Touch the touch sensor outside. The door locks.

If you have no card

  1. Go out and close the door.
  2. Touch the touch sensor outside.
  3. Put your finger on the scanner within 15 seconds. The door locks.

If the door is locked and you are inside

  1. Tap your card on the card reader inside the room. The door opens.
  2. Go out and close the door.
  3. Touch the touch sensor outside to lock it again.

Emergency (no internet)

  1. Tap the emergency card on the card reader inside the room. The door opens.
  2. Go out and close the door.
  3. Touch the touch sensor outside to lock it again.
Touching the touch sensor only will not lock the door.
